# Copied from the README Label Taxonomy. baseline.py reads these as the prompt.

system: The writer has settled on an account of a game, and the post spells out at least one checkable rule of play: an input, a cost, a lock, or a condition that decides whether an action succeeds.
verdict: The writer has settled on a judgment, of a game or of a way of playing, and the post does not spell out a rule of play.
ask: The writer is still asking the sub what to do, or what the rule for quitting should be, and the post would not stand as a finished account if the question were removed.
